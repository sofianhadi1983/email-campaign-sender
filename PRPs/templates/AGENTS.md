# PRP Templates

## Purpose

- Provide reusable structure for creating context-rich, verifiable PRPs.

## Ownership

- `prp_base.md` owns the default PRP outline and quality gates.

## Local Contracts

- Templates must retain sections for goals, context, implementation, validation, and final checks.
- Placeholder content must remain visibly distinguishable from concrete requirements.

## Work Guidance

- Keep template guidance technology-neutral unless an example is explicitly labeled.

## Verification

- Confirm required sections remain present after template edits.
